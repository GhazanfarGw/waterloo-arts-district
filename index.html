About Waterloo

Vineyards, rail yards, then rock and roll.

Collinwood grew up on grapes and trains, filled with Irish, Italian and Slovenian families, and lost much of that when the factories closed. Cheap rents in the 1990s brought artists and musicians back, and the street has been rebuilding around them ever since.

1870Grape capital+

Collamer, later Collinwood, was North America’s largest grape shipping point.

1890Railroad town+

A major switching point for the Lake Shore & Michigan Southern Railroad.

1900sHalls and homes+

The Beachland began as the Croatian Liberty Hall; the Treelawn fills the old Slovenian Workmen’s Home.

1990sArtists move in+

Affordable rents draw young people, artists and the underground music scene.

2002Waterloo Arts forms+

Seed money from The Cleveland Foundation starts the nonprofit; the Arts Fest follows soon after.

2026A full calendar+

Brite Winter moves to Waterloo and the Arts Fest turns 23.
